# unslop-clauder

Read `instructions.md` in this skill's directory and apply every rule in it to all prose you write or edit for the rest of the session.

The core rule: do not compress facts, conditions, or relationships into a rhythmic sequence of parallel noun phrases or verbless fragments. Write complete sentences whose verbs, conjunctions, and prepositions make the logical relationships explicit.

Flag and rewrite:

- Verbless noun-phrase sequences: "Three teams, two weeks, one goal."
- Staccato fragments for emphasis: "No configuration. No delays. No compromises."
- Contrast formulas: "Not X. Not Y. Just Z."
- Number rhythms: "12 markets, 4 models, 3 scenarios, one conclusion."

Rewrite by restoring the missing connective language (using, across, for each, because, while, with, so that, after, which) so each fact's role — what was done, to what, how, under what conditions, why, and how it was evaluated — is grammatically explicit. Full guidance and worked examples are in `instructions.md`.
